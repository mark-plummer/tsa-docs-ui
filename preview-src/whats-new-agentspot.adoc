[#oct-01-2026]
== October 1, 2026

30 updates, including a new Discover tab, a Microsoft 365 connector, and a redesigned workflow run experience.

* *Discover shared work across your org:* a new Discover tab ranks the agents, apps, and workflows your organization already uses.
* *Microsoft 365 connector:* bring Outlook, Calendar, OneDrive, SharePoint, Excel, and Teams into your agents and workflows.
* *Memory privacy controls:* turn personal memory off, and let organizations turn memory off tenant-wide.
* *Workflow runs:* a redesigned run page with an outcome-first summary, connector health, and live progress updates.
* *Data Apps renamed to Apps* throughout the product.

[#sep-11-2026]
== September 11, 2026

11 updates, including the ability to cancel workflow runs and continuous Slack conversations.

* *Cancel workflow runs* while they are in progress.
* *Continuous Slack conversations:* AgentSpot now remembers the conversation in Slack DM and channel threads.
* *More reliable workflows:* large lists and parallel steps no longer fail the entire run, and workflows can include up to 50 managed agents.
* *More consistent memory* across an entire conversation.

[#sep-01-2026]
== September 1, 2026

14 updates, including actions in AgentSpot apps and login hint support for single sign-on.

* *Actions in apps:* apps can now run actions end to end, triggering follow-up steps and writing data back.
* *Tableau connector authentication support,* a step toward connecting your Tableau data to AgentSpot apps.
* *Login hint support for single sign-on:* returning users see their email pre-filled at login.
* *More reliable workflow connector tools* and clearer style options in the app builder.
